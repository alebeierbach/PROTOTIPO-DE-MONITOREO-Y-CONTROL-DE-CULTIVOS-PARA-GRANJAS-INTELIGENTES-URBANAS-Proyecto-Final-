\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This Integrative Project, developed within the LIADE research line on urban agriculture, presents the design, construction, and experimental validation of a smart vertical urban farm prototype. A modular structure was built around a Raspberry Pi 4 as the central processing unit, automating irrigation, LED lighting, and ventilation for soil-grown arugula and spinach based on real-time environmental sensing. The control logic, implemented in Python, regulates temperature, humidity, and the daily light integral (DLI) received by each crop, while cloud telemetry enables remote monitoring under an IoT scheme. Experimental trials over several months confirmed stable crop development, and the low duty cycle of the high-power actuators kept the system's average energy consumption at a modest level.
